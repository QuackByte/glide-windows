Glide's privacy policy

Glide doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. Your settings, shortcuts, app rules and window layout stay on this PC, in %APPDATA%\Glide. Glide keeps a log in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Glide, which also stays on this PC.

Update checks: to keep itself up to date, Glide asks GitHub (api.github.com) twice a day whether there's a newer release, and downloads it from GitHub when you install it. That request carries Glide's version and, like any request, your IP address; nothing else is sent. GitHub handles it under its privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

You can turn update checks off on the next page of this installer, or later in Glide's Settings, under Updates.

Full policy: https://quackbyte.dev/glide/privacy/
